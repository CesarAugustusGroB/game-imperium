Deployment

Your legate brings base discipline and a pool of formations (common + trait-unlocked). Discipline unlocks formations and the complex orders within them. Pick ONE — it stays locked for the whole battle.

100% 10,000 HP · mass stats ×1.00 the march thins the ranks: HP + charge/harass/push/siege scale down (movement holds)
10.0 / 10 inherited from the campaign — and written back when the battle ends
Tiered center: dominating the position climbs tiers and each tier grows your die +1 face (d6→d9, up to +3) — affects damage and checks. Pushing takes the center (bigger die + type bonus) but exposes you to flanking (crits). · Skirmish = safe damage but cedes the center and is blunted by armor · costs ammo. · Charge = impact + recoil: a braced line halts it; breaks weakened morale (finisher). · Siege pierces armor and fortification. · Maneuver = envelop / flank.
